ABOUT COLORCLOUDS

A learning brand built
for a changing world.

Colorclouds is the mother brand behind a growing ecosystem of learning products, experiences, content and education programs.

We believe learning becomes stronger when children are invited to play, discover, create, reflect and grow — rather than simply consume information.

Our work is designed to connect creative learning with real-world opportunities for publishers, schools, organisations, community partners and education-focused businesses.
