Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the Colorclouds Affiliate Program.

What is the Colorclouds Affiliate Program?

Recommend Colorclouds learning products to your audience and earn commission on qualifying purchases made through your approved referral link.

Who can become a Colorclouds Affiliate?

Parents, educators, content creators, bloggers, publishers and community builders are welcome to apply. Applications are subject to review and approval.

Does it cost anything to join?

There is no application fee. Our team will share the program terms and next steps before you join.

How do I earn?

Once approved, share your unique affiliate link. Qualifying purchases attributed to your link earn commission according to the program terms.

Where can I share my affiliate links?

Share links on your website, blog, social channels or with your community, following the program guidelines and clearly disclosing your affiliate relationship.

Can I promote Colorclouds on Instagram and YouTube?

Yes. Include your social profiles in your application and tell us how you plan to recommend Colorclouds products.

How do I track my referrals?

Referral tracking and reporting details will be shared with you when your application is approved.

When will I receive my commission?

The commission rate, payout schedule and payment method will be explained in the program terms before you join.

Are all purchases eligible for commission?

Commission eligibility follows the program terms. Returns, cancellations and other exclusions may affect the commission earned.

Can I apply if I am a small creator?

Yes. Tell us about your audience and your ideas for sharing Colorclouds. Every application is reviewed individually.
